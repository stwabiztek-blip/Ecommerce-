This dashboard provides a visual analysis of product sales and order performance. It highlights total revenue, 
order volume by product category, and the distribution of order statuses. 
The report helps understand top-performing items and overall order workflow insights.


1️⃣ Total Sales by Product

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Metric: Sum of Order Amount

Description:
Displays the total sales revenue generated by each product.

Products like Jacket, Smartphone, Coffee Beans, and Headphones are shown as the top revenue contributors.

Lower-performing items include Organic Juice and Sofa.

2️⃣ Total Orders by Product

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Metric: Count of Orders

Description:
Shows how many orders were placed for each product.

Highest order volume recorded for Jacket, Smartphone, and Coffee Beans.

Items with fewer orders include Organic Juice and Sofa.

3️⃣ Order Status Summary

Chart Type: Category Bar Chart

Metric: Count of Orders by Status

Statuses Displayed:

Completed: 275 orders

Cancelled: 260 orders

Pending: 246 orders

Returned: 219 orders

Description:
Gives an overall summary of how orders are progressing across different stages.

📌 Key Insights

Jackets and Smartphones consistently lead in both revenue and order count.

Completed orders are the highest, indicating good fulfilment efficiency.

Notable pending and cancelled orders highlight areas for process improvement.

Products like Sofa and Organic Juice may need marketing or sales strategy review due to lower engagement.

📁 Files & Usage

These visuals can be used for:

Sales presentations

Management reporting

Business strategy planning

Performance monitoring

🛠️ Tools Used

Power BI / Excel (Assumed)

Sales dataset with fields: ProductName, OrderID, OrderStatus, OrderAmount
